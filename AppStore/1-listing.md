Jumbo — это английский язык в игре, для детей и для всех, кто хочет учить слова весело и каждый день. Рядом с вами слонёнок Jumbo: он радуется вашим успехам и поддерживает, если что-то не получилось.

**Description:**
```
Jumbo is English as a game, for children and for anyone who wants to learn words in a fun way every day. Jumbo the little elephant is with you: he cheers your wins and encourages you when something goes wrong.

HOW IT WORKS
• 10 books, from the beginner level A1 to B2. Every book has 20 topics, every topic has 15 words: 3000 words in all.
• Topics follow a snake path: finish a topic and the next one opens.
• Every topic has three games, a video lesson and a "Topic words" icon with the word list, sound and translation.

GAMES
• "Find me" - find the word by the picture.
• "Listen and find" - hear a word and pick it.
• "Build the phrase" - put an English phrase together from words, using the translation.
• "Remember the order" and "Step by step" train your memory.
• For the alphabet and numbers: "Listen and tap" and "The missing wagon".

THE WHOLE ALPHABET AND A DICTIONARY
• The alphabet and the numbers up to 20 with sound are open from the start.
• A dictionary of all the words of the books: search, translation, examples, sound.
• "Review words" helps you come back to what you have already learned.

MOTIVATION
• A daily goal and a streak of days in a row.
• Lives in the games: a mistake costs a life. When the lives run out, mistakes take LP points.
• Awards for books and levels, LP points.
• A game market: spend points on frames, badges and useful things (a shield, a streak freeze). Everything is paid with points inside the game, never with money.
• A weekly rating of your team, school and region. You can hide your name in the rating.

FOR SCHOOLS AND TEACHERS
Pupils join the team (class) of their school. The teacher sees the progress of the team and the exam results.

FRIENDLY TO CHILDREN
• No ads, no analytics, no trackers.
• A phone number is optional.
• You can delete the account at any time in Settings.

App languages: Русский, English, O'zbekcha.

Questions: kholmatov.javlon@gmail.com
Website: jumbo.uz
```
